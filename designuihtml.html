Bridging the Digital Accessibility Gap

Why traditional e-commerce leaves millions behind

Older shoppers, regional-language speakers, and busy everyday buyers shouldn't have to wrestle with search bars, filters, or multi-screen checkouts.

Delayed Merchant Replies

Customers message a business on WhatsApp, wait hours for manual price checks, and abandon their purchase.

Complex Catalogs & Forms

Navigating websites, typing exact English spellings, and filling checkout forms creates friction for everyday buyers.

Rigid "Press 1" Chatbots

Menu-tree bots break the moment a customer asks a contextual question or sends a mixed grocery list in one message.

Disconnected Stock & RTO

Selling out-of-stock items causes refunds, while unverified COD orders trigger costly Return-to-Origin (RTO) losses.

One Shared Engine · Two High-Impact Modes

Built for Direct Basket Checkout & High-Ticket Lead Sales

Merchants can activate Commerce Mode, Lead Mode, or both on the exact same WhatsApp Business number.

Commerce Mode (Modules A–H)

From Inquiry to Verified Payment

For Kirana stores, restaurants, D2C brands, pharmacies, and wholesale distributors.

  • WhatsApp Native Flows & Catalogs: Interactive product cards, delivery slot pickers, and address forms rendered natively inside WhatsApp.
  • Live Stock Reservation: Reserves inventory at checkout and revalidates before order creation so two buyers never claim the last unit.
  • RTO Shield Risk Scoring: Flags high-risk COD orders (incomplete addresses, repeat cancellations) and nudges prepaid UPI incentives.
  • Probable Cart Abandonment Recovery: Detects whether a buyer dropped due to price or delivery fees and sends compliant template follow-ups.
Lead Mode (Module I)

From Enquiry to Booked Appointment

For Real Estate, Education, Insurance, Auto Dealers, and Travel Agencies.

  • Instant 24/7 Lead Qualification: Replies in seconds in the lead's language, asking one short question at a time for budget, need, and timeline.
  • Google Calendar Slot Booking: Checks merchant availability, proposes 2–3 free slots via interactive buttons, and prevents double-booking.
  • Automated Stop-Aware Follow-ups: Timed follow-up sequences (2h, 1d, 3d, 7d) that halt the instant a lead replies, books, or opts out.
  • Regulated Vertical Guardrails: Strict forbidden-claim rules ensure insurance or real estate bots never promise returns or unapproved policies.
